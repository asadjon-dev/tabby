# Tabby privacy policy

Tabby does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- Your wallpaper, widgets, notes, bookmarks, colors and font choices are saved only in this browser, on this device.
- Tabby makes no network requests unless you add a Weather widget. Then it sends the city name you search for, and that city's coordinates, to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the forecast. Nothing else is sent, and no account or ID is used. Tabby has no analytics, no accounts, no ads and no remote code.
- Tabby asks for one browser permission, "favicon", to show your bookmarked sites' icons from the icons your browser already stores. Turn it off in Settings → Site icons.
- When you click a bookmark or run a search from the search widget, your browser opens that website. That visit is between you and the website.
- Backup files you export (they can include your widgets, settings, uploaded font and wallpaper, whatever you tick) are saved to your computer and are never uploaded. Restoring a backup reads the file on your computer only.
